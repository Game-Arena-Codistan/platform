# Legal and Provider Approval Checklist

The repository contains implementation drafts, not legal advice. Public launch requires recorded approval for every item below.

## Operator and contacts

- Legal operator name and registered/business address
- Public support email and operating hours
- Privacy/contact email and security-reporting email
- Effective date and versioning process for notices

## Player terms and privacy

- Minimum age and guardian-consent approach
- Privacy notice, processor list, data locations and retention schedule
- Account export/deletion process and statutory record exceptions
- Terms, acceptable use, suspension, refund, liability, dispute and governing-law wording
- Accessibility/support commitments

## Premium and payments

- JazzCash merchant agreement and production credentials
- Approved checkout fields, callback/return URLs and settlement process
- Refund/cancellation policy and support ownership
- Written decision on recurring billing. Do not describe monthly/yearly access as automatic renewal unless JazzCash and the merchant agreement explicitly support it and checkout obtains clear consent.
- Tax/accounting treatment and invoice/receipt requirements

## Games and content

- Ownership or distribution licence for every launch game and artwork asset
- Permission to host/mirror original builds on the controlled origin
- Age/content suitability and any required warnings
- Third-party SDK, font, audio, image and trademark rights
- Takedown and rights-holder contact process

## Rewards and competitions

- Arena Coins non-cash/non-transferable disclosure
- Challenge and tournament eligibility, tie-break, integrity, cancellation and dispute rules
- Confirmation that initial rewards do not create prohibited wagering, chance or cash-prize activity

## Evidence

Record approver, role, date, approved document/version, conditions and renewal/review date. Store signed provider/legal documents outside the public repository and reference only their approved identifiers.
