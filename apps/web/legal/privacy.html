Privacy notice

Game Arena uses account identifiers, game activity, premium entitlement records, payment references, device diagnostics and support information to operate the service. Wallet PINs and full card details are handled by the payment provider and are not collected by Game Arena.

Current frontend preview

This release candidate stores preferences, favourites, recent games and demo session state in your browser. Clearing site data removes that local information.

Production requirements

The final notice will describe retention periods, analytics consent, account deletion, data export, service providers and support contacts after the backend and payment contracts are finalized.
