Privacy notice

This notice describes the data Game Arena is designed to use. The final operator identity, support contact, effective date, processors and jurisdiction-specific wording must be approved before public launch.

Data we use

  • Account data: verified mobile number or email, account ID, display name and identity-verification timestamps.
  • Security data: hashed OTP challenges, session and device records, IP/device risk hashes, rate-limit events and audit records.
  • Game activity: games opened, play sessions, version, duration, submitted score, verification status, challenges, tournament entry and Arena Coin ledger entries.
  • Premium and payment data: plan, amount, transaction status, JazzCash reference, callback status and entitlement history. Game Arena does not collect a JazzCash MPIN, wallet PIN or full card details.
  • Preferences: favourites, recent games, Data Saver, motion, sound and analytics choice stored in the browser.
  • Optional analytics: de-identified product events, performance measurements and bounded error messages only after the player enables Optional analytics. Phone, email, OTP, token, payment and session fields are blocked from these events.

Why we use it

We use data to sign players in, secure accounts, deliver games, verify scores and rewards, manage premium access, process and reconcile payments, operate competitions, provide support, detect abuse, improve reliability and meet accounting or legal obligations.

Service providers

Data may be processed by contracted hosting, database, content-delivery, OTP, email, payment, monitoring and support providers only for the service they provide. The final provider list and data locations must be recorded before launch.

Retention

  • OTP challenges expire after five minutes; abuse-control events are retained only as needed to enforce limits and investigate attacks.
  • Sessions normally expire within 30 days and can be revoked from the account page.
  • Account and gameplay data remains while the account is active. A deletion request signs out all devices and begins the configured deletion process, currently 30 days.
  • Payment, settlement, fraud and audit records may need a longer retention period required by the provider, accounting, tax or law. The approved schedule will be published before launch.

Player choices

Players can disable optional analytics, download an account export, sign out devices and request account deletion from Account. Browser preferences can also be removed by clearing site data.

Children and families

Some games may be suitable for younger players. A parent or guardian should supervise a minor’s use. The final minimum age, guardian-consent and child-safety wording must be approved for the launch market.

Contact and complaints

The operator name, privacy contact and support address will be inserted after owner and legal approval. Security issues must follow the private reporting process in the project security policy rather than a public issue.
