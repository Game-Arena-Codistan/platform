General rules

Arena Coins and challenges

Arena Coins

Arena Coins are loyalty points only. They have no cash value, cannot be withdrawn, transferred, sold, exchanged between players, staked or wagered. A displayed balance is calculated from an append-only server ledger.

How rewards are verified

A game requests a reward after a server-created play session. Game Arena checks the game and version, session nonce, score, duration, completion rate, duplicate results, account eligibility and daily limits. A game cannot credit coins directly.

Premium multiplier

An active Game Arena+ entitlement may provide the displayed 2× reward rate. The multiplier is calculated by the server when a valid result is accepted. It does not apply after premium expires or while a payment remains pending.

Limits and review

Daily caps, per-game cooldowns and challenge limits may apply. Suspicious or technically inconsistent results can be placed in review instead of being credited. Game Arena may reverse a duplicate, fraudulent or erroneous entry with an attributable audit record.

Challenges

A challenge must show its start and end time, eligible games or versions, target, premium requirement and reward before a claim. Progress recorded outside the challenge window or on a non-eligible version does not count. One reward is available per account unless the challenge states otherwise.

Changes

Future rewards may change, but a change will not reduce an already accepted ledger entry. Material changes to an active challenge should be announced or the challenge should be cancelled fairly.

Pre-launch legal and consumer-disclosure review is required.
