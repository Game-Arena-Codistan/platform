General rules

Game Arena tournaments

These rules apply together with the event-specific details shown before entry.

Eligibility

Players need a verified account and must meet the displayed age, location, premium and device requirements. One entry per account applies unless an event explicitly allows more.

Event definition

Every event must identify the eligible game and version, entry window, play window, scoring method, maximum attempts, tie-break, reward, result-verification method and any player limit.

Scoring and tie-breaks

Only server-accepted score events count. Unless an event states otherwise, higher score ranks first, then shorter verified duration, then earlier accepted completion time.

Fair play

Automation, modified builds, replayed messages, account sharing, score manipulation, network interception, multi-account entry and exploiting a known defect are prohibited. A result may be reviewed, delayed, rejected or disqualified. Material decisions must be recorded in the audit trail.

Technical failure

Game Arena may pause, extend, rerun or cancel an event when a widespread game, platform, provider or connectivity failure makes the result materially unfair. Individual connectivity or device problems do not automatically create another attempt unless the event rules say so.

Rewards

Current tournament rewards are Arena Coins unless the event explicitly states another approved reward. Arena Coins have no cash value. Cash, wagering and chance-based prizes are not part of the initial service.

Results and disputes

Provisional results may be held for integrity review. The event page must state the dispute window and support route. The final support contact and dispute process require owner/legal approval before launch.

Pre-launch legal and consumer-disclosure review is required.
